icchipuchuu Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

icchipuchuu helps neighbors share items for free, swap items through barter, and coordinate local direct-cash exchanges.

2. Location & Listing Data

  • Location Access: If you tap to update your location, your device may request coarse or fine location permission so nearby listings and exchange areas can be shown.
  • Listings & Preferences: Item titles, exchange modes (Free Share, Barter Swap, Direct Cash), and notes you enter are stored for app functionality.

3. Data Deletion & User Control

You can clear locally stored listing data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling icchipuchuu. For data removal or privacy questions, contact the developer through your Google Play Store listing support contact.
